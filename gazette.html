Preseason primer

Post-draft. Prices from Sleeper. No invented rankings.

Eight chairs at Haute Poppies. One hundred twenty-eight picks. One unused dollar. The inaugural Sunday Scaries auction is in the books, and the room told on itself.

This is an 8-team half-PPR superflex. Starting lineup is 1 QB, 2 RB, 2 WR, 1 TE, 2 FLEX, 1 SUPERFLEX, 1 K, 1 DEF, plus five bench. That format makes quarterbacks scarce and running backs expensive. The board followed both rules, then broke a few others.

How the room spent $1,598

Zach Cabra left $2 on the table. Everybody else went $200 even.

Running back was the tax. Thirty-eight RBs sold for $580. Jahmyr Gibbs went for $61 to Connor Zetsche, the high pick of the night. Bijan Robinson went $56 to Brad Bell. Saquon Barkley went $42, also to Brad. Christian McCaffrey went $39 to Barry Rockwood. The room decided workhorse backs still win 8-team leagues.

Wide receiver was the depth position. Forty-five WRs sold for $509. The top of that market was not Chase. It was Jaxon Smith-Njigba at $42 to Ben Bankhead, then Amon-Ra St. Brown at $41 to Zach, then Puka Nacua at $39 to Kevin Kutch. Ja'Marr Chase went $33 to Will Maness. If Chase is still Chase, that is the quietest value on the board.

Quarterback split the room. Twenty-one QBs sold for $376. Jacob Williamson paid the superflex tax out loud: Josh Allen $49, Lamar Jackson $38, Matthew Stafford $13. Brad took Burrow at $33 and Jaxson Dart at $22. Barry took Patrick Mahomes at $10 and stopped. That is the bet to watch.

Tight end died after two names. Trey McBride $24, Brock Bowers $24, then Tyler Warren $10 and Colston Loveland $16. Kickers and defenses were leftovers, except Brandon Aubrey at $13.

The eight rosters

Connor Zetsche, Crazy Rich Achanes, $200. Stars and scraps. Gibbs $61, Achane $35, CeeDee Lamb $33, Dak Prescott $36. That is $165 on four players. After that it is Tyler Warren, Caleb Williams, Jameson Williams, and a row of $1s. If Gibbs and Achane both hit, the name on the team is a warning label. If one of them slips, the bench is not coming to save it.

Brad Bell, $200. Two running backs and a prayer. Bijan $56 plus Saquon $42 is $98 before he paid Burrow $33 and Dart $22. Ladd McConkey at $13 is the only wide receiver who cost real money. Brian Thomas, Diggs, Addison, and Makai Lemon were $1 each. The Superflex is covered. The flex spots are a weekly argument.

Jacob Williamson, Jake from StateFarm, $200. The quarterback factory. Allen, Lamar, Stafford, and Jordan Love for $101. Ashton Jeanty $34 and A.J. Brown $24 are the skill-position spine. After that the running backs are $1 dart throws. In this format the QB room can carry a thin backfield. It has to.

Ben Bankhead, BankCity, $200. The cleanest sheet. JSN $42, James Cook $40, Omarion Hampton $29, Jayden Daniels $25, Brock Bowers $24, Rashee Rice $15. No $60 monument. No empty position. Bo Nix at $8 is the extra starter. This is the roster that does not need a story.

Kevin Kutch, $200. Drake Maye $42, Puka $39, Derrick Henry $35, Brock Purdy $22, Chris Olave $20. Then a wall of wide receivers: DeVonta Smith, Mike Evans, Terry McLaurin, DK Metcalf. Tight end is Tucker Kraft and Mark Andrews for $4 combined. The skill depth is real. The position scarcity is at TE.

Will Maness, Lizard King, $200. Spread the money. Chase $33, Kenneth Walker $31, George Pickens $28, Chase Brown $25, Malik Nabers $20. Five players between $20 and $33 instead of one $60 chip. Quarterback is Trevor Lawrence $17 with Kyler and Baker behind him at $3. Travis Kelce at $2 is a dart, not a plan. The roster is deep and a little quarterback-light for a superflex.

Zach Cabra, $198. Two alpha receivers. Amon-Ra $41 and Justin Jefferson $32, then Drake London $15. Jalen Hurts $26 and Justin Herbert $17 cover the Superflex. The running back after Jeremiyah Love at $24 is Rhamondre, Henderson, and Dobbins. Aubrey at $13 is the luxury pick of the night. The unused $2 does not matter. The thin backfield might.

Barry Rockwood, Rock ’em Sock ’em, $200. Christian McCaffrey $39, Trey McBride $24, then a pile of usable backs: Kyren, Javonte, Skattebo, Breece, Bucky. Nico Collins, Zay Flowers, and Jaylen Waddle are the wide receiver core. The hole is the one that matters in this league. Patrick Mahomes at $10 is the only quarterback. In a superflex with two flexes, that is a season-long tightrope.

What is actually in play

Buy-in is $30 through Sleeper. Pot is $240. First place 80 percent. Second 20 percent. Weekly low score shotguns a beer, selfies it, and sends it to the group. Toilet Bowl is lose-and-advance for seeds 5 through 8. Last place does a modified NFL combine. No keepers this year.

Have Fun and Don’t Be a Dick. Lineups lock soon enough.
